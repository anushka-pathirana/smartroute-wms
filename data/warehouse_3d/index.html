How to use

Left-drag to rotate, right-drag to pan, scroll to zoom. Hover a box for its code; click it for details. Each box is one storage location; boxes are stacked by rack level (1 = floor). Coordinates are drawing units of the authors' CAD layout (1 unit = 0.07625 m).
Teal = I/O point (depot, CC-08)  |  grey strips = aisles
